I am a Ph.D. student in the [Augment Research Group](https://wms.cs.kuleuven.be/cs/onderzoek/augment) at the Department of Computer Science, KU Leuven, supervised by [Prof. Katrien Verbert](https://wms.cs.kuleuven.be/cs/onderzoek/augment/katrien-verbert) and [Dr. Yucheng Jin](https://yucheng.gold/). I have also been fortunate to collaborate with [Dr. Zhen Wang](https://zhenwang9102.github.io/).

My research explores bidirectional alignment between humans and large language models. I am particularly interested in how models represent and understand individual users, and how to support interaction with AI that suits individual needs and preferences. More broadly, I aim to build language models that better understand people and design interactions that help users better understand and control these models.
