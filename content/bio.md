I am a Ph.D. student in the [Augment Research Group](https://wms.cs.kuleuven.be/cs/onderzoek/augment) at the Department of Computer Science, KU Leuven and [imec](https://www.imec-int.com/en), supervised by [Prof. Katrien Verbert](https://wms.cs.kuleuven.be/cs/onderzoek/augment/katrien-verbert) and [Dr. Yucheng Jin](https://yucheng.gold/). I have also been fortunate to collaborate with [Dr. Zhen Wang](https://zhenwang9102.github.io/).

My research focuses on how large language models represent and understand individual users, alongside interaction design that supports diverse needs and preferences. More broadly, I aim to make LLMs more personalized, transparent, and controllable for users.
